Automated Minutes of Meeting Generator
 Synthetic Training Dataset — Conversation → Structured MoM
This dataset is designed for training and evaluating an automated meeting-minutes system. Each record contains a realistic meeting
transcript and a reference annotation covering summary, decisions, action items, risks, and next-meeting information. Conversations
intentionally include interruptions, informal language, follow-up questions, ambiguous context, and domain-specific terminology.
Dataset size: 15 meetings across software, marketing, finance, sales, research, AI, security, HR, and other scenarios.

Recommended Training Format
For supervised fine-tuning, a practical input/output pair can be formed from each meeting. Input: the raw transcript plus an instruction
such as 'Generate concise meeting minutes.' Target: the structured reference annotation. For larger experiments, create several
variants of each meeting with different levels of transcript noise, speaker interruptions, transcription errors, and different output
formats.
Suggested JSON fields: meeting_id, transcript, participants, summary, decisions, action_items, risks, next_meeting, minutes.
